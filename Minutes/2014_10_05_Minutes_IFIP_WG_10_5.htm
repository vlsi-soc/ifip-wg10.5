Status of preparation of VLSI-SoC 2015 by Kiyoung Choi

     (see attached slides)

 

Information on slides is not repeated here.

Daejeon is 2 hours by express train from the international airport.

It is the center of a technological area.

 

VLSI-SOC will take place in the convention center, which has been reserved for the IFIP World Congress. 30 K$ in the budget will be paid to IFIP for the rooms. The Grand Ball Room, too big for VLSI-SoC, will not be used.

 

It is clarified that IFIP provides the 6500€ to support the travel expenses of PhD students from developing countries (list of countries from IFIP). So this is in addition to the budget, not part of it. But the budget can add more support to PhD students if money is available.

 

The printed Call for Papers is distributed to the participants, and to VLSI-SOC attendees.

It will be distributed also to ICCAD, ICCD, DATE, and other events that WG members attend.
