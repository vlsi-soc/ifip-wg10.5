WG Awards Committee

 

Three IFIP awards are now open:

 

(1) The IFIP Fellow Award, presented every year. IFIP's most prestigious technical distinction. Conferred to a current or past member of an IFIP body in recognition of outstanding contributions in the field of Information Processing as a Technical Leader, Scientist, Engineer or Educator.

 

The EC has decided to extend the deadline for nominations to June 15th, 2019.

 

Up to 48 Fellow awards may be made in 2019. The nomination process for Fellows is considerably more complex and time-consuming than for other awards so starting early is strongly recommended. As well as completing a nomination form you will need to collect forms completed by five referees and (optionally) one or two 'endorsers' and upload them all with the nomination form. Referees and endorsers need not be members of the GA. Proposers for Fellow also need not be GA members. Details of who may act as proposer, referee and endorser are given on the website.

We will do the same as last year for the nomination.

 

(2)   The Silver Core: Deadline for nominations is July 15th, 2019.

 

(3)   The Service Award: Deadline for nominations is July 15th, 2019.
