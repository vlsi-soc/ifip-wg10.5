5 - Status of preparation of VLSI-SoC 2014

 

Luc Claesen, Program Chair, provides the list of TPC members for VLSI-SoC 2014 (there are some double TPC members in the tracks as indicated by Prof. Masahiro Fujita, but this is currently being updated). See attached file tpc.pdf.

 

The schedule is re-worked during the meeting, as follows:

deadline bidding for papers by TPC members: 25 April 2014

deadline review hand-in: 30 May 2014

paper selection decision by track chairs: 9 June2014

globalization of program: 17 June 2014

notification of acceptance to authors: 18 June 2014

camera ready papers: 10 July 2014

conference: 6 - 8 October 2014

 

The updated call is attached: see file VLSISoC2014CFPn.pdf

 

The program will include special sessions: 3 keynotes + 1 panel + 4 submitted special sessions organized by Salvador Mir

 

Other points

  • As a matter of smoothing the paper submission and evaluation process, it is recommended to use the paid version of Easychair: include in the budget around 250 British pounds for 150 papers
  • It is a requirement that one of the authors must present the paper as a condition for having the paper published at IEEEXPlore.
  • The Web-site should be updated to include the TPC and new schedules. The layout needs to be improved. A link to the web-sites of previous conferences should be added.
  • As in previous years the program will include special sessions: 3 keynotes + 1 panel + up to 4 submitted special sessions organized by Salvador Mir
  • A special track for managing the "conflict of interest papers" for the track chairs will be added. These papers will be reviewed by members of the track where they were submitted, but the Program Chair will manage the track.
