Status of preparation of VLSI-SoC 2015 by Kiyoung Choi

     ( see attached slides)

 

  • 10 Tracks
  • 138 abstracts received
  • 118 papers received (lower than expected); potentially due to conflict with ESWEEK
  • Acceptance Rate:
    • Regular: 41%
    • Regular + Poster: 56%
  • PhD Forum
