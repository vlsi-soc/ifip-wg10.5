Minutes of the IFIP WG 10.5 Meeting
February 26th, 1998
DATE, Paris

At our working group meeting on 26 February 1998 that we had in Paris we elected a new working group chair: Prof. Jean Mermet.

In the next coming months he will set up a team of other officers for the working group. His term starts in effect from after the working group meeting.

The working group members decided that IFIP Working group 10.5 would like to participate to DATE (Design, Automation and Test in Europe) starting for the next conference in 1999. In order to be able to participate, we need to provide the payment for this sponsorship in the next few months. This matter will be further taken up by the new working group chair.
